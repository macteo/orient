# orient

A simple Italian website that turns the IOF control-description and ISOM
references into flash cards and quizzes with a verdict, for one family to
memorise orienteering symbols.

**Current focus:** Level 1 — Vision & Scope — all six decisions confirmed, level review written.
**Next step:** Founder signs off the Level 1 review in
[01-vision/_index.md](01-vision/_index.md); then Level 2 opens with hosting
and frontend strategy.
