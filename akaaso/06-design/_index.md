# Level 6 — Screen Design

**Status:** Signed off 2026-09-05 — five screens approved by the founder in the canvas; enforcement strict
**Started:** 2026-09-05
**Last updated:** 2026-09-05

Screens are the deduplicated union of the routes named in Level 4 and Level
5: five routes, five screens. Their states are derived from the story
variants collected in [05-stories/_coverage.md](../05-stories/_coverage.md),
including six outside the canonical vocabulary (`retro`, `inversa`,
`verdetto`, `ripresa`, `nessun-errore`, `ripasso`,
`conferma-cancellazione`), each declared by a story and justified in its
screen file. The artboards are generated by
[scripts/build-artboards.py](../scripts/build-artboards.py) in the dialect
of the founder's draft `Orient.dc.html`, on the Braisor - Preline design
system, with real artwork embedded (Purple Pen pictograms, an IOF ISOM
drawing, one worked example). The founder's draft stays in the canvas as the
seed; it is not a screen and is not in this table.

Accent resolved: `--tint` is set once to Preline blue (`--blue-600`) in the
artboards' helmet and, at implementation, in the app stylesheet
([2.006](../02-architecture/006-design-direction.md)).

## Review

Review in the canvas: project *Orient site base scaffold*, the nineteen
`.dc.html` files at its root. Each run screen has a `stato` property (the
state shown) and a `mazzo` property (the deck whose card face is shown);
the home has `modo`, `carte`, `nessunaSezione`, `conRisultati`. Canvas
comments cannot be read from this side: paste the text of a review into
the conversation, or write it into a `<!-- DESIGN NOTES: … -->` block at
the top of the artboard.

## Open Questions

None. The founder reviewed the canvas on 2026-09-05 ("per me va tutto
abbastanza bene") after two generator fixes: a method placed outside the
component class (a syntax error the canvas reported on
`[mazzo].risultati.empty`), and pictograms clipped by a wrong outer
`viewBox`; the reverse-quiz row tiles were also widened to full width.
The canvas needs its own reload button after a re-upload; a blank artboard
after a push is cache, not a defect.
