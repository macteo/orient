### T2 — Results in the browser

Not a conflict between sources but a revision under the skill's own rule:
"we cannot proceed without changing this". The founder's instruction
("tieni i risultati sul client: sul browser dell'utente") reversed the Level
1 exclusion of browser storage. The blast radius was six decisions across
three levels, all amended the same day with change-log lines; the
no-personal-data rule survived untouched because the storage is anonymous
and never leaves the device.

### T1 — Unlicensed control-description artwork on a public site

The two map specifications carry a Creative Commons notice on page 2; the
control descriptions document ends with an impressum and no licence. The
brief wants the site online. The founder accepted the risk for family use
on 2026-09-05 and [1.005](../01-vision/005-constraints.md) names the exits.
Later that day the pictogram artwork was sourced from Purple Pen ([S4],
BSD) and the ISOM artwork from the IOF's own illustration package ([S5],
CC BY-ND), so the residual risk was limited to [S3]'s Italian text. Level 3
then widened the use of [S3] — page-3 rows, about a hundred worked
examples with their map clips and sketches, their sentences — and the
founder re-confirmed the risk for that scope on 2026-09-05
([3.008](../03-modules/008-security-compliance-and-recovery.md)). Reopens
only if the audience widens.
