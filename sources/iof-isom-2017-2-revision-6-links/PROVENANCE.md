# Provenance

- Source: the IOF Map Commission's "IOF Mapping documents package" for national
  map commissions, linked from https://orienteering.sport/iof/mapping/
  ("National map commissions can download ... from here"), a Dropbox share
  owned by the IOF Map Commission. Folder `IOF_ISOM_2017-2_Revision_6/Links/`,
  fetched 2026-09-05 as a zip (14.0 MB).
- Contents kept: every `ISOM *.pdf` — one vector PDF per ISOM 2017-2 symbol
  (`ISOM 109 Small knoll.pdf`), plus the definition figures (`ISOM Def_*.pdf`),
  the minimum-dimension figures (`ISOM min_dim_*.pdf`), `ISOM Screens.pdf`,
  `ISOM Running speed.pdf`, and `by-nd.eps`, the Creative Commons BY-ND badge
  the specification prints on its licence page.
- Not kept: IOF logo files (trademarks, not needed).
- Licence: ISOM 2017-2 is published under CC BY-ND 4.0 (the badge above; the
  notice on page 2 of the specification, mirrored in [S1]). These files are
  the specification's own illustrations, and each carries the red dimension
  annotations and blue "min." boxes of the printed page. Reproduction is
  verbatim with attribution; the content pipeline (Level 3) crops or masks
  the annotations without redrawing the symbol.
- The same package exists for ISSprOM 2019-2 Revision 6 (deferred deck).
